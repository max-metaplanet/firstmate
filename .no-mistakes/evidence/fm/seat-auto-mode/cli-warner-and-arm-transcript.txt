## S10 usage-warner setter, percent LEFT, same shape as fm-seat.sh threshold
$ fm-usage-warner.sh threshold
(unset - no window is watched, so this home warns about nothing)
[exit 0]
$ fm-usage-warner.sh threshold five_hour 20
threshold: five_hour at 20% left (which is 80% used)
[exit 0]
$ fm-usage-warner.sh threshold seven_day 10
threshold: seven_day at 10% left (which is 90% used)
[exit 0]
$ fm-usage-warner.sh threshold five_hour 150
fm-usage-warner: percent left must be a whole number from 1 to 100, or "off"
[exit 1]
$ fm-usage-warner.sh threshold
five_hour	20% left (warns once at or below 20% left, which is 80% used)
seven_day	10% left (warns once at or below 10% left, which is 90% used)
[exit 0]
--- config/usage-warner:
five_hour:20
seven_day:10
$ fm-usage-warner.sh threshold seven_day off
threshold cleared: seven_day
[exit 0]
$ fm-usage-warner.sh threshold
five_hour	20% left (warns once at or below 20% left, which is 80% used)
[exit 0]
